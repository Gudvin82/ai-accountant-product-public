# Market Context

## Why Now

Accounting outsourcing faces growing operational complexity:

- more documents and intake channels;
- higher expectations for speed and quality;
- increasing cost of manual processing;
- harder team standardization;
- clients expect faster responses;
- AI tools are more available, but they need control and evidence.

## Why AI Fits This Domain

Accounting workflows include many repeatable tasks:

- field extraction;
- document classification;
- payment matching;
- discrepancy detection;
- drafting client questions;
- drafting journal entries;
- deadline control.

At the same time, accounting is a regulated domain. AI should therefore be
introduced as an assistant with a review gate, not as an autonomous decision
maker.

## Why A Generic Chat Is Not Enough

A generic AI chat does not know:

- where the source document is;
- who approved the decision;
- which payment is linked to the document;
- which rule version was used;
- what has already been sent to the client;
- where the audit trail is.

The product value is not "chat for accountants". It is an operating platform
around documents, operations, tasks, and approvals.

## Proper Market Positioning

AI Accountant is better described as:

- AI accounting assistant;
- AI accounting copilot;
- operating layer for accounting outsourcing;
- human-reviewed accounting automation;
- document and reconciliation intelligence platform.

Not as autonomous accounting.
