# For Partners

## Who This Discussion Fits

AI Accountant may be relevant if you have:

- accounting outsourcing operations;
- multiple clients or legal entities;
- recurring primary-document flow;
- bank statements and manual reconciliation;
- tasks, deadlines, and client questions across different channels;
- interest in testing an AI assistant without losing human control.

## Pilot Format

A proper pilot should be limited:

- 1-3 clients or one internal workflow;
- one accounting period;
- agreed document types;
- anonymized or approved data;
- manual approval for every AI suggestion;
- baseline measurement before and after.

## What Helps For A Product Discussion

Useful inputs:

- list of document types;
- sample bank statement without sensitive data;
- description of the current process;
- list of frequent discrepancy classes;
- who approves decisions;
- systems in use: 1C, EDI, banks, email, CRM;
- data storage and transfer constraints.

## What Is Not Needed At The First Step

- real passwords or tokens;
- full 1C access;
- real client documents without approval;
- mass integration with every system;
- replacing the current accounting process.

## Pilot Goal

The pilot should answer:

- whether the system saves accountant time;
- which documents are recognized reliably;
- which discrepancies are detected automatically;
- where AI fails;
- how much manual review remains;
- which architecture is needed for production.
