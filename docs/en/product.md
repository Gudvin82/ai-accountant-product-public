# Product Description

## Short Version

**AI Accountant** is an operations platform concept for accounting outsourcing
teams. It helps manage many clients in one workspace: documents, bank
operations, reconciliation, discrepancies, tasks, deadlines, AI suggestions, and
approval history.

The product does not replace 1C or any accounting system of record. It works as
an operational layer above existing processes: collecting data, preparing
evidence, detecting issues, and helping accountants reach a reviewed result
faster.

## Problem

Accounting outsourcing teams lose a lot of time on operational friction rather
than expert accounting:

- documents arrive through different channels;
- bank statements come in different formats;
- accountants manually match payments to documents;
- discrepancies get lost in chats;
- managers cannot easily see workload and risks;
- client questions are not always tracked as controlled tasks;
- generic AI tools often provide answers without sources or responsibility
boundaries.

## Solution

AI Accountant creates one operating contour:

1. The accountant selects a client.
2. Documents, invoices, acts, universal transfer documents, receipts, or bank
   statements are uploaded.
3. The system extracts fields, amounts, dates, tax IDs, counterparties, document
   type, and confidence.
4. Bank operations are normalized and matched to documents.
5. Discrepancies become tasks.
6. AI drafts journal entries, client questions, and possible explanations.
7. The accountant approves, corrects, or rejects.
8. The manager sees deadlines, workload, quality, and bottlenecks.

## Product Positioning

AI Accountant is not a "magic accountant". It is a **control layer for an
accounting team**, where AI supports preparation and review instead of making
final decisions.

Core principle:

> AI suggests, the accountant confirms.

This matters for trust, accountability, and real accounting practice.

## Target Users

- Accounting outsourcing firms.
- Small and medium accounting teams.
- Team leads managing many legal entities.
- Companies where documents, payments, and client questions currently live
  across messengers, email, 1C, spreadsheets, and folders.
- Teams that want an AI pilot without losing human control.

## Main Modules

- Clients and organizations.
- Primary documents.
- OCR and structured extraction.
- Bank statements and operations.
- Document-to-payment reconciliation.
- Discrepancies and tasks.
- Draft journal entries.
- Human approval.
- Audit trail and decision history.
- AI assistant with client and document context.
- Manager control: deadlines, workload, quality.
- Future integrations: 1C, EDI, banks, tax services, email, CRM.

## Differentiation

- It does not try to replace the accountant.
- It does not finalize important outcomes without human confirmation.
- It shows source, confidence, and history.
- It treats accounting as a team operation, not as a single AI chat.
- It preserves the path from source document to approved decision.
- It fits the outsourcing model where one accountant manages many clients and a
  manager needs operational control.
