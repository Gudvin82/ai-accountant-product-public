# Product Limitations

## What AI Accountant Does Not Do

AI Accountant should not:

- replace a qualified accountant;
- submit reports autonomously;
- make final tax or legally significant decisions;
- guarantee absence of errors;
- send data to 1C, EDI, banks, or tax services without an approved process;
- use normative conclusions without sources and effective dates;
- store real documents without an approved production security contour;
- send sensitive data to unknown AI/OCR providers.

## Why Limitations Matter

In accounting software, trust is built not by promising that "AI will do
everything", but by control:

- where the data came from;
- what was extracted;
- how confident the system is;
- who approved the outcome;
- what was sent downstream;
- how the decision can be verified later.

## Public Repository Boundary

This repository:

- describes the product concept;
- shows the architecture direction;
- records the roadmap;
- does not contain production source code;
- is not a legal opinion;
- does not claim that all integrations are ready.

## Pilot Boundary

A pilot should run only under controlled conditions:

- limited number of clients;
- anonymized or explicitly approved data;
- manual review of every AI suggestion;
- assigned accountant responsibility;
- isolated credentials and providers;
- no autonomous accounting decisions.
