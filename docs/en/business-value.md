# Business Value

## For Accountants

AI Accountant reduces manual operational work:

- process incoming documents faster;
- search less for payment-to-document matches;
- see disputed fields before they become errors;
- receive draft journal entries and client questions;
- work from one client context instead of folders and chats.

Core value: accountants spend more time reviewing and deciding, and less time
searching and moving data around.

## For Accounting Team Leads

Managers gain operational visibility:

- which clients are at risk;
- where documents are missing;
- where deadlines are overdue;
- which tasks are stuck;
- where AI often fails;
- how accountants are loaded;
- how much time a period takes to process.

Core value: management is based on evidence instead of manual status updates in
messengers.

## For Accounting Outsourcing Firms

For an outsourcing company, the product acts as a quality-control layer:

- fewer lost primary documents;
- fewer unmatched payments;
- faster period-close preparation;
- clear decision history;
- visibility for managers;
- foundation for scaling the team without operational chaos.

## For The Outsourcing Client

The client receives clearer communication:

- questions are tied to specific documents and operations;
- fewer repeated clarifications;
- clearer requirements before period close;
- missing documents are detected earlier.

## How To Prove Value

A pilot should measure:

- time per document;
- time per reconciliation item;
- accepted AI suggestions;
- corrected OCR fields;
- detected discrepancies;
- overdue tasks;
- processing cost per client.

Without verified pilot metrics, the product should not promise a specific
efficiency percentage.
